MODULE 3: Merkle Tree Builder

Binary Merkle Tree Construction, Student Certificate Grouping & Proof Verification

💡 What happens when you click "Build Merkle Tree"?

When you generate the Merkle Tree, Module 3 executes a 3-step cryptographic grouping process:

  1. Step 1 (Leaf Level): Retrieves the Module 2 SHA-256 hash for every student certificate in the batch (e.g. Rahul Sharma, Priya Patel, Amit Verma, Sneha Kulkarni).
  2. Step 2 (Pair & Combine): Combines hashes in pairs (e.g. Hash(Rahul) + Hash(Priya)) and hashes the combined string to form Parent Nodes.
  3. Step 3 (Merkle Root): Repeats pairing upwards level-by-level until ONE single master 64-character Merkle Root is generated at the top!
